Prepare your cues

  1. Create a show on the Shows tab.
  2. Open its cue list and choose Import audio from Files. Select an owned, unprotected WAV, AIFF, MP3 or M4A file.
  3. Use the settings control beside a cue to name it, add an operator note and set gain and fade duration. Reorder the list when playback is stopped.
  4. Select the cue you want ready, then return to Stage and check the cue name, number, next cue and connected output before GO.

Play deliberately

Only one cue plays at a time. GO is disabled while preparing, playing or fading. A brief rapid-tap guard prevents an accidental second GO. Natural completion advances to the next ready cue without starting it. Stop keeps the interrupted cue ready for replay. Fade & next ends the sound over its chosen fade, then advances. Rehearsal mode holds the same selected cue instead of advancing.

After an interruption or audio-output disconnect/change, playback is held. Check the room, connected output and volume, then choose Arm this cue. There is no automatic resume. The current sound may finish while the app is backgrounded; another cue will not start automatically.

Save and restore a complete backup

  1. Stop playback and choose Back up complete show from Show actions.
  2. Choose Save to Files or another share destination. Keep the original ZIP unchanged.
  3. To restore, open Shows and choose Restore a show ZIP. StageTap checks the package and audio before adopting it.
  4. Restoration creates a new show, never overwrites an existing one. The free library holds one show: keep its backup, then move the original to recoverable Trash before restoring if you need space. Lifetime Pro adds more shows.

To recover a trashed show, use More → Recoverable Trash. Permanent deletion cannot be undone. Removing the app also removes its local library; keep a separate ZIP backup first.

Free and Lifetime Pro

Free includes one complete show, ten cues, all transport controls, rehearsal mode and portable backup. Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It adds up to ten local shows, one hundred cues per show and ten reusable cue-setting templates. Attach audio to template-based shows separately. The purchase sheet shows Apple’s localized price. Restore Purchases uses the same Apple Account that purchased Pro.

Existing shows remain playable, editable and exportable if Pro cannot be verified. Adding above free capacity requires a verified Pro entitlement. If the price cannot load, your free show still works; check your connection and try loading again. Pending purchases unlock only when Apple verifies them.

Media and storage limits

Audio files must be local, unprotected, no larger than 100 MB or 60 minutes, mono or stereo, at 8–192 kHz. A show can contain up to 750 MB of audio; the library including recoverable Trash has a 2 GB limit. Damaged or unsupported files are rejected before replacing accepted audio. Streaming music, DRM, audio overlap, DMX/MIDI, timecode and live microphone mixing are outside V1.

Run a complete rehearsal on the actual speaker, headphones or connected output you intend to use. Check device volume as well as cue gain. Keep a backup and an independent plan for live use. StageTap does not provide a professional failover system.
