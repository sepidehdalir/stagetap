Privacy policy · updated October 20, 2026

Your sounds
stay with you.

StageTap is provided by Arvinify / Sepideh Javanbakht Dalir. This policy describes the native app and these support pages.

Local app content

The app processes imported audio, cue names, operator notes, show files and templates on your device. There is no developer account, developer backend, advertising, tracking SDK or analytics uploader. The app does not record the microphone or request access to your photo library.

Files and backups you choose

You select files to import and destinations for saving or sharing a show ZIP. An imported audio file is copied into the local library. A complete show backup includes its cue list, notes and original encoded audio; original audio tags or metadata may remain. Review what you share and choose a destination you trust.

Files in iCloud Drive or another provider follow that provider’s settings and policies. Device backups are controlled by your Apple settings. The app does not automatically upload show content to the developer. Library content remains until you delete it or remove the app; keep an external show backup. Recoverable Trash remains local until you permanently delete it.

Purchases

Apple handles payments and StoreKit transaction verification. StageTap uses verified entitlement information to unlock Lifetime Pro. The developer does not receive payment card details or operate a purchase backend for this app.

Support

If you email support, the information you choose to send is used to answer your request. Do not send private audio unless you want it reviewed. Contact hello@arvinify.com for support or privacy questions.

These web pages

These pages do not include developer analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels or forms. GitHub hosts the pages and may process website request information under its privacy policy. Opening a support page does not upload your app’s audio or show library.

Updates

This page will be updated if the app’s data practices change. The date above identifies this version.
